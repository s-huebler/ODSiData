Add GVHD prophylaxis to the harmonized GVHD metadata.

Follow the "Harmonization Variable Additions" section of CLAUDE.md for all
standing conventions.

SHAPE: categorical covariate (two columns, two axes)

DEFINITION: The pharmacological regimen given to prevent GVHD, started at or
around transplant. This is prophylaxis given to everyone as standard of care —
it is NOT treatment given after GVHD is diagnosed, and the two must not be
conflated. Every regimen observed across these studies is a calcineurin
inhibitor backbone plus a partner agent, so I want the partner agent and the
backbone identity as separate columns: methotrexate causes mucositis and gut
epithelial damage and mycophenolate is enterohepatically recycled, so the
partner agent is the axis most likely to act on the gut microbiome, while
cyclosporine vs tacrolimus is a weaker direct effect I still want recoverable.

CANONICAL VOCABULARY:

  gvhd_prophylaxis       cni-mtx | cni-mmf | cni-steroid | cni-alone | other
    cni-mtx      calcineurin inhibitor + methotrexate
    cni-mmf      calcineurin inhibitor + mycophenolate mofetil
    cni-steroid  calcineurin inhibitor + corticosteroids
    cni-alone    calcineurin inhibitor monotherapy
    other        regimen recorded but composition not specified

  gvhd_prophylaxis_cni   cyclosporine | tacrolimus
    NA where the regimen is recorded but the backbone is unspecified.

PLACEMENT: immediately after sample_day_rel_transplant and before the agvhd
block, so the column order reads patient characteristics -> sample timing ->
treatment exposure -> outcomes.

PER-STUDY CONSTRUCTION:
- Artacho:  not recorded -> NA for both columns. There is no prophylaxis column
            in the source at all.
- DAmico:   not recorded -> NA for both columns. Conditioning_regimen is the
            PREPARATIVE regimen, not prophylaxis — do not map it. See NOTES.
- Fujimoto: not recorded -> NA for both columns. AGVHD_Treatment is treatment of
            established aGVHD, not prophylaxis — do not map it. See NOTES.
- Ingham:   gvhd_prophylaxis (3 levels) ->
              "Cyclosporine A + Methotrexate"   -> cni-mtx
              "Cyclosporine + Corticosteroids"  -> cni-steroid
              "Cyclosporine A"                  -> cni-alone
            gvhd_prophylaxis_cni = cyclosporine for all three.
            The one row with no clinical metadata stays NA.
- Liu2017:  Collapse the five one-hot columns. cyclosporine/tacrolimus give
            gvhd_prophylaxis_cni; methotrexate/mmf/sirolimus give the partner
            agent (sirolimus is constant "no" in this cohort). Donor rows -> NA
            for both columns, as with every other Liu clinical variable.
            Assert in the tests that every recipient has exactly one CNI and
            exactly one partner agent — this holds in the current data, and a
            violation means the one-hot expansion has broken.
- Vallet:   gvhd_proph_type ->
              "ciclosporin-MTX"  -> cni-mtx,  cni = cyclosporine
              "cyclosporin-MMF"  -> cni-mmf,  cni = cyclosporine
              "Other"            -> other,    cni = NA
            Note the source spells it "ciclosporin" in one value and
            "cyclosporin" in the other; match both.

NOTES:
- Coverage is only 3 of 6 studies (271 of 885 samples). Record the three
  not-recorded studies explicitly in the decision log so the NAs are a logged
  decision rather than an oversight.
- Two tempting wrong mappings, both of which I want explicitly ruled out in the
  code comments: DAmico's Conditioning_regimen contains EDX (cyclophosphamide),
  which is CONDITIONING cyclophosphamide and not post-transplant cyclophosphamide
  prophylaxis; and Fujimoto's AGVHD_Treatment contains mPSL and ATG, which are
  rescue therapy after aGVHD was diagnosed. Neither belongs in a prophylaxis
  column.
- Ingham also has X197ATGmm (ATG given, n=72) and
  Was.graft.manipulated.for.GVHD.prophylaxis (constant 0). I have deliberately
  left both out of this variable — see the stop-and-ask below.
