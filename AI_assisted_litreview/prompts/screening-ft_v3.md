You are doing second-pass full-text screening for the ODSiData scoping review
("Gut Microbiome and Time-to-GVHD in Adult Allo-SCT Recipients"). You are
invoked **once per PDF** by a bash wrapper. Each invocation: read one PDF,
classify it, find the matching row in the input CSV, append that row to the
output CSV with classification tags added to the `Tags` column. Do **not**
modify the input CSV. Do **not** re-process PDFs already in the output CSV
(the wrapper handles dedup, but verify before writing).

One of the core goals of this review is to assemble an **analytic
subcohort** at the end of screening — i.e., a set of studies whose raw
microbiome + outcome data we can re-analyze ourselves. This means that a
paper failing our outcome criterion on its own writing can still be valuable
if the authors clearly collected GVHD data and have made their microbiome
data accessible. The `PossibleDataSource` edge-case override (Step 3, below)
exists to catch these papers.

## Step 1 — Read the PDF

Use `pdftotext -layout PDF_PATH -` (or `pdfplumber` for stubborn layouts).
Extract enough text to make a confident decision . Always extract the full methods section.

**Also extract the Data Availability statement** (often near the end of the
paper, before references; sometimes a numbered footnote, sometimes a
standalone heading like "Data Availability", "Accession Numbers",
"Sequence Data Deposition", or "Materials and Methods → Data Sharing").
This is needed for the `PossibleDataSource` edge-case check in Step 3.

If the PDF cannot be parsed (corrupt, image-only, password-protected),
classify as `Exclude` with reason `NoFullText` and skip to Step 4. Do not OCR
unless the PDF text is empty.

---

## Step 2 — Match the PDF to its CSV row

The PDF filenames (e.g. `Nat. Med.-2017.pdf`, `1954699.pdf`) do **not**
reliably match any CSV column. Match by **DOI first, PMID second, title
last**:

1. Extract DOI from PDF text. Patterns: `10.\d{4,9}/[-._;()/:A-Z0-9]+` (case
   insensitive). Strip URL prefixes (`https?://(dx\.)?doi\.org/`). Lowercase
   the result.
2. Extract PMID. Patterns: `PMID:\s*(\d+)`, `PubMed\s+ID:\s*(\d+)`. Bare
   8-digit numbers are not reliable on their own.
3. Look up in `INPUT_CSV`:
   - DOI match: compare lowercased PDF DOI against lowercased `doi` column
     (also try the `Url` column, which contains `http://dx.doi.org/...`).
   - PMID match: compare against the `pmid` column as a fallback.
   - Title fallback: normalize whitespace and lowercase the first ~60 chars
     of the PDF title line; compare against the `Title` column.

If no row matches, **do not write anything** to `OUTPUT_CSV`. Print one
line to stderr in the form `UNMATCHED PDF_PATH (doi=..., pmid=..., title=...)`
and exit successfully so the wrapper continues. I will reconcile unmatched
PDFs manually.

## Step 3 — Classify the paper

A paper is **Include** if ALL three criteria hold for the paper as a whole
**OR** for any clearly defined subset of its cohort/analysis. The "any
subset" rule: a paper with a 144-patient cohort where only 50 patients had
stool 16S is includable on the strength of that 50-patient subgroup.

"All criteria met" means **no criterion is explicitly violated**. Soft or
caveated satisfaction of a single criterion does not block inclusion but
should push the paper into the `EdgeCase-Include` bucket. Explicit violation
of a criterion pushes to `Exclude` (or `EdgeCase-Exclude` if borderline).

### Criterion 1 — COHORT

Human patients undergoing allogeneic stem-cell, hematopoietic-cell, or
bone-marrow transplantation: allo-SCT / allo-HSCT / allo-HCT / allo-BMT.
Pediatric and adult both qualify. Hematologic-malignancy indication is
typical but not required.

- Mixed allogeneic + autologous cohorts qualify if the allogeneic subset is
  separable in the analysis.
- **Autologous-only cohorts do NOT qualify.**
- **Humanized mouse models do NOT satisfy the cohort criterion**, even when
  the title uses the word "human" or the cells are human-derived. Mouse arms
  are acceptable only when there is also a qualifying human patient cohort
  in the same paper. Do not be misled by "human" in the title — verify the
  in vivo cohort in the Methods.

### Criterion 2 — GUT MICROBIOME EXPOSURE, MEASURED

The gut, intestinal, or fecal microbiome must be **measured** in the study
cohort (or in a subset). Acceptable measurement signals, strongest first:

- 16S rRNA sequencing of stool / intestinal samples
- Shotgun metagenomics of stool / intestinal samples
- qPCR of named gut taxa from stool
- Alpha/beta diversity computed from stool
- Named gut taxon abundance reported as a finding
- SCFA / butyrate / bile-acid metabolite panels, treated as microbial readouts
- Surveillance cultures of stool and/or rectal swabs (and other gut-derived
  specimens) for named gut organisms — e.g. periodic colonization screening
  for VRE, ESBL- or carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales, carbapenem-
  resistant *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, MRSA, or other "alert" pathogens.
  Colonization status counts as a microbiome measurement whether the result
  is positive or negative, and whether the cultures were collected
  prospectively or pulled retrospectively from infection-control records.
- Clinical microbiology records of stool-based enteric pathogen testing
  identifying named gut organisms — e.g. *C. difficile* toxin / PCR assays,
  stool culture for bacterial enteric pathogens, protozoal stool tests,
  enteric viral PCR panels. These count even when the testing was clinically
  indicated (e.g. workup of post-transplant diarrhoea) rather than collected
  under a research protocol, as long as the paper's analysis ties the
  resulting microbiological findings back to the allo-SCT cohort.

**Non-gut sites alone do NOT satisfy this criterion**: bacteremia /
bloodstream cultures, vaginal, oral, skin, lung. A paper whose primary site
is non-gut still qualifies if it **also** explicitly measures or reports
observed gut/intestinal dysbiosis. A study that measures only bloodstream
bacterial cultures or only the vaginal microbiome — even in the right allo-SCT
cohort with a GVHD outcome — fails Criterion 2 and should be excluded with
reason `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome`.

If alpha/beta diversity or named taxa are reported but the sequencing
platform / measurement modality is never named, classify as `EdgeCase-Include`
with reason `DiversityMetricsOnly`.

If the only gut-microbiome measurement in the paper is restricted to a
non-bacterial kingdom — most often the fungal mycobiome (ITS sequencing,
fungal qPCR, fungal culture panels) but also archaea-only or virome-only
work — classify as `EdgeCase-Include` with reason `MycobiomeOnly` (use that
literal tag even for archaea-only or virome-only papers; treat it as the
catch-all for non-bacterial-only gut microbiome characterization). The
paper is still potentially useful but does not contribute bacterial 16S /
shotgun data to the eventual analytic cohort.

For purposes of the `PossibleDataSource` edge-case override below, note
which signals are present. The override requires **taxonomic classification**
specifically — 16S rRNA sequencing, shotgun metagenomics, qPCR of named
taxa, or reported abundance of named taxa. Diversity metrics alone or
metabolite panels alone do **not** clear that override's bar even if they
satisfy Criterion 2 generally.

### Criterion 3 — GVHD OUTCOME

Acute or chronic GVHD must be discussed as an outcome, covariate, exposure,
or substantive mechanistic focus relating to the intestinal microbiome.

- Organ-specific GVHD subtypes all qualify: enteric / gastrointestinal,
  hepatic, vulvar-vaginal, cutaneous, ocular, etc.
- Broader allo-SCT outcomes (overall survival, TRM, infection, engraftment,
  relapse) qualify **only when the gut microbiome is itself the substantive
  focus** of the work.
- A single passing mention of GVHD is not enough — GVHD must be
  substantively engaged with.

**Important interaction with the `PossibleDataSource` override:** a paper
that fails this criterion because GVHD is mentioned only as a covariate or
in a supplemental no-association analysis is **not** automatically hard-
excluded. Before assigning `Exclude` + `NoGVHDOutcome`, check the
`PossibleDataSource` override below. If it applies, route to
`EdgeCase-Exclude` instead so the paper surfaces for manual review as a
candidate raw-data source.

### Study-type rules

| Study type | Include if … |
|---|---|
| Primary investigation (RCT, cohort, case-control, case series) | All three criteria met for the paper or any clear subset. |
| Meta-analysis / systematic review | Synthesis explicitly combines microbiome measurements (taxa or diversity) with GVHD as outcome, covariate, or exposure. |
| Narrative review | GVHD is discussed substantively (not in passing) AND the gut microbiome is given a substantive mechanistic role (specific taxa, diversity, or measurable mechanism named). Reviewed papers may themselves be reviews or primary investigations. |
| Mechanism / therapeutic paper (FMT, MSC, prebiotic, antibiotic, etc.) | Motivating hypothesis links a specific gut microbial taxon or community state to GVHD AND the gut microbiome is **measured**. If the intervention targets the gut microbiome but no microbiome measurement is reported (e.g. FMT case study with no 16S), classify as `EdgeCase-Exclude` with reason `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome`. |
| Pure opinion / perspective with only passing microbiome mention | Exclude `PassingMentionOnly`. |
| Trial protocol / study design paper with no pilot or feasibility data | `Exclude` with reason `StudyProtocol`. Use this even when the protocol describes a future microbiome measurement plan and a future GVHD outcome — without data, the paper cannot enter the analytic cohort. Do **not** use `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome` for these (it implies the data are missing in error rather than not-yet-collected by design). |
| Correction notice / erratum / publisher's correction with no original data | `Exclude` with reason `CorrectionNotice`. The original paper is screened on its own merits separately. |

Exclude any paper identified as a Conference Abstract, Meeting Summary, or Poster Presentation. These are typically characterized by a lack of a full "Methods" or "Results" section and often appear in supplemental journal issues.
Identify an abstract by looking for header/footer text containing "Abstracts from the [Year] Meeting," "Supplement," or "Poster Session." In the text, look for "Trial Registration" or "Funding" appearing immediately after a very brief results paragraph without detailed tables or figures. Classify as Exclude with reason ConferenceAbstract. These are distinct from short reports. Do NOT exclude "Brief Reports," "Short Communications," or "Letter to the Editor" (if it contains original data) based on length alone. 

### Edge-case overrides (these route to one of the two edge-case buckets)

- **Non-English full text** → **always** assign `EdgeCase-Include` or
  `EdgeCase-Exclude` (whichever the content supports) with reason `Language`.
  Combine with any content-based reason if applicable.
- **Case study (n=1)** with the right cohort and outcome → `EdgeCase-Include`
  with reason `CaseStudy`. If the microbiome is also unmeasured →
  `EdgeCase-Exclude` with `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome` + `CaseStudy`.
- **PDF unreadable / truncated / missing** → `Exclude` with reason `NoFullText`.
- **`PossibleDataSource` (analytic-subcohort candidate)** — see below.

#### `PossibleDataSource` override

This override applies to papers that would otherwise be hard-excluded for
`NoGVHDOutcome` (and only that reason) but appear to be re-analyzable from
their raw data. A paper qualifies for `EdgeCase-Exclude` +
`PossibleDataSource` if **ALL FOUR** of the following hold:

1. **Cohort.** Criterion 1 is met for the paper or for a clearly defined
   subset (allo-SCT / allo-HSCT / allo-HCT / allo-BMT).
2. **Taxonomic microbiome measurement.** The paper reports microbiome data
   at the level of named taxa or community classification — 16S rRNA
   sequencing, shotgun metagenomics, qPCR of named taxa, or named-taxon
   abundance as a primary readout. Diversity metrics or SCFA/metabolite
   panels alone do **not** satisfy this prong (a diversity-only paper that
   otherwise qualifies should be handled under `DiversityMetricsOnly`).
3. **Accessible data.** The paper explicitly indicates the underlying
   sequencing / microbiome data are available. Acceptable signals:
   - A sequence-read accession number (SRA `SRP…`/`PRJNA…`, ENA `PRJEB…`,
     GenBank, MG-RAST, QIITA study ID, EBI MGnify).
   - A controlled-access repository deposit (dbGaP `phs…`, EGA `EGAS…`).
   - A named biorepository / sample bank with the implication that samples
     or sequence data can be requested.
   - A "Data Availability" statement promising data on reasonable request
     **with** a corresponding deposit reference or repository named.
   A bare "data available on request" with no repository or accession is
   **not** sufficient.
4. **GVHD was measured, even if not analyzed in depth.** The text must
   indicate that GVHD data were *collected* on this cohort. Acceptable
   signals (any one suffices):
   - GVHD appears as a covariate in a Table 1–style cohort description with
     incidence numbers or grading.
   - The Methods state that GVHD was prospectively recorded, graded, or
     adjudicated (e.g. "acute GVHD was graded using consensus criteria").
   - A secondary or supplemental analysis examines GVHD as outcome or
     exposure, even with a null finding (e.g. Lee 2017's Cox model of CDI →
     subsequent GVHD with no association reported).
   - A supplemental table or figure (referenced in the main text) reports
     GVHD incidence or timing.
   A single passing mention of "GVHD" in the introduction or discussion
   with no indication that the cohort's GVHD status was actually recorded
   is **not** sufficient.

If all four hold, route the paper to `EdgeCase-Exclude` with **both**
`SecondPassAI-ExcludeReason-NoGVHDOutcome` **and**
`SecondPassAI-EdgeCaseReason-PossibleDataSource`. In the rationale (Step 5
stdout line), name the accession number / repository found and quote the
sentence indicating GVHD was measured.

This override does **not** apply when a different exclusion reason is
operative (e.g. `AutologousOnly`, `NonHuman`, `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome`,
`NoFullText`, `ConferenceAbstract`). It rescues only `NoGVHDOutcome` /
`PassingMentionOnly`-on-GVHD excludes.

### Decision tree summary

1. PDF unreadable? → `Exclude` + `NoFullText`.
2. Conference abstract / poster? → `Exclude` + `ConferenceAbstract`.
3. Non-English? → route to `EdgeCase-Include` or `EdgeCase-Exclude` based on
   content, always tagged `Language`.
4. Otherwise, evaluate the three criteria:
   - All three clearly met → `Include`.
   - All three met but with one soft / caveated → `EdgeCase-Include` with
     reason naming the soft criterion.
   - One criterion explicitly violated but the paper is borderline (e.g.
     intervention targets the gut but no measurement) → `EdgeCase-Exclude`.
   - **Only Criterion 3 (GVHD) violated, and the `PossibleDataSource`
     override applies** → `EdgeCase-Exclude` +
     `ExcludeReason-NoGVHDOutcome` + `EdgeCaseReason-PossibleDataSource`.
   - One or more criteria clearly violated and no override applies →
     `Exclude`.

## Step 4 — Build the tag string

The output `Tags` column = the original `Tags` value from `INPUT_CSV`,
followed by a comma and the appended classification tags (also
comma-separated). Preserve any pre-existing Papers tags like `Prompt Gen`,
`Screen1`, `Screen2.1` — append, do not overwrite.

Tag format (use these literal strings, normalized exactly as below):

**Decision tag — exactly one:**

- `SecondPassAI-Include`
- `SecondPassAI-Exclude`
- `SecondPassAI-EdgeCase-Include`
- `SecondPassAI-EdgeCase-Exclude`

**Reason tag(s) — required for any decision except clean `Include`:**

- For `Exclude` or `EdgeCase-Exclude`: one or more
  `SecondPassAI-ExcludeReason-<Reason>` tags.
- For `EdgeCase-Include` or `EdgeCase-Exclude`: one or more
  `SecondPassAI-EdgeCaseReason-<Reason>` tags describing why the paper is
  edge-case (e.g. `Language`, `CaseStudy`, `SubsetOnly`, `DiversityMetricsOnly`,
  `PossibleDataSource`).
- `EdgeCase-Exclude` papers may carry BOTH `ExcludeReason-` and
  `EdgeCaseReason-` tags — the exclude reason names the violated criterion,
  the edge-case reason names why this is still borderline. The
  `PossibleDataSource` case is the canonical example:
  `SecondPassAI-EdgeCase-Exclude, SecondPassAI-ExcludeReason-NoGVHDOutcome,
  SecondPassAI-EdgeCaseReason-PossibleDataSource`.

### Controlled reason vocabulary (seed list)

Use a fragment from this table whenever it applies. **Do not invent novel
reasons unless none of these fit.** If a paper truly needs a new reason,
generate it in `CamelCase` and **also** add the literal flag tag
`SecondPassAI-NovelReason` so I can review and fold the new reason into the
vocab.

| Reason fragment            | Applies to            | Trigger |
|----------------------------|-----------------------|---------|
| `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome`  | Exclude / EdgeCase-Exclude | Cohort + outcome OK, but gut microbiome itself never measured. |
| `NonHuman`                 | Exclude               | Mouse / animal / humanized-mouse only; no human patient cohort. |
| `AutologousOnly`           | Exclude               | All transplants are autologous. |
| `WrongCohort`              | Exclude               | Cohort not allo-SCT/HSCT/HCT/BMT at all. |
| `NoGVHDOutcome`            | Exclude / EdgeCase-Exclude | GVHD never engaged with as outcome / covariate / focus. Pair with `EdgeCaseReason-PossibleDataSource` when the override in Step 3 applies. |
| `PassingMentionOnly`       | Exclude               | GVHD or gut microbiome present only as a passing mention. |
| `NoFullText`               | Exclude               | PDF unparseable or missing. |
| `Language`                 | EdgeCaseReason        | Full text in language other than English. |
| `CaseStudy`                | EdgeCaseReason        | n=1 case report. |
| `GVHDNotPrimary`           | EdgeCaseReason        | Microbiome studied in allo-SCT cohort but GVHD is only a secondary mention. |
| `DiversityMetricsOnly`     | EdgeCaseReason        | Diversity metrics / named taxa reported but no sequencing modality named. |
| `PossibleDataSource`       | EdgeCaseReason        | Paper fails Criterion 3 on its own writing but has the right cohort, taxonomic microbiome data, an accessible data deposit (accession / biorepo), and evidence GVHD was measured. Candidate for the analytic subcohort via raw-data re-analysis. Always paired with `ExcludeReason-NoGVHDOutcome`. |
| `ConferenceAbstract`       | Exclude               | Full text is identified as an abstract or conference talk or poster. |
| `StudyProtocol`            | Exclude               | Trial protocol / study design paper with no pilot or feasibility data. Use in place of `UnmeasuredGutMicrobiome` for protocols. |
| `CorrectionNotice`         | Exclude               | Erratum / publisher's correction with no original data. The corrected paper is screened separately. |
| `MycobiomeOnly`            | EdgeCaseReason        | Only non-bacterial gut-microbiome measurement (typically fungal mycobiome via ITS / fungal qPCR / fungal culture; also use for archaea-only or virome-only). Routes to `EdgeCase-Include`. |

## Step 5 — Write the row to `OUTPUT_CSV`

1. Before writing, confirm the row's `item ID (Read-Only)` is not already
   present in column 1 of `OUTPUT_CSV`. If it is, exit without writing.
2. Read the entire matched row verbatim from `INPUT_CSV`. Preserve field
   order, original quoting, and any embedded commas/newlines.
3. Replace the value of the `Tags` field with: `<original_tags>, <new_tags>`
   where `<new_tags>` is the comma-separated decision + reason tags from
   Step 4.
4. Append the modified row to `OUTPUT_CSV` (do **not** rewrite the header).
   Use a CSV-aware writer (`python -c "import csv; ..."` or `csvkit`) so
   commas and quotes inside field values are preserved correctly.
5. Print one line to stdout: `<citekey> -> <decision_tag> [<reason_tags>]`.
   For `PossibleDataSource` cases, also append a parenthetical noting the
   accession / repository identifier found and a short quote (≤25 words)
   showing GVHD was measured.
