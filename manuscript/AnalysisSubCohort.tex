\section{Fujimoto2024}
This study was investigating whether a newly developed anti-\textit{Enterococcus faecalis} enzyme could be used to target a specific type of pathogenic \textit{Enterococcus faecalis} to reduce the chance of GVHD. This study motivates our work, showcasing the downstream scientific pipeline of therapeutics once microbiome targets are identified.  \\
Important scientific background:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textit{Enterococcus faecalis} dysbiosis is a GVHD risk factor 
    \item \textit{E. faecalis}
\end{itemize}
