# Paredes_2026 — paper framework

Full title: "The intestinal microbiota as a key modulator of acute graft-versus-host disease" (Paredes, Schmiester, Jenq, van den Brink; Nature Reviews Cancer, 2026).

## Mechanism structure
Four top-level sections (confirmed on p1 TOC):
1. Introduction
2. The intestinal microbiota and allo-HCT
3. Mechanisms of dysbiosis-mediated GVHD
4. Clinical interventions
5. Conclusion and future perspectives

Within section 3, mechanisms are organized by (a) immune interplay, (b) metabolite signaling (SCFAs, bile acids, indoles, aromatic amino acids, etc.), (c) bile acid signaling, (d) virome/mycobiome (Box 1).
