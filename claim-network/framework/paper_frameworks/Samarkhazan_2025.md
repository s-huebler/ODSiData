# Samarkhazan_2025 — paper framework

Full title: "The microbiome in graft-versus-host disease: a tale of two ecosystems" (Soleimani Samarkhazan, Nouri, Maleknia, Aghaei; Journal of Translational Medicine, 2025, 23:832).

## Mechanism structure
Narrative review framing GVHD microbiome biology around two ecosystems: the recipient's (disrupted) microbiome and the donor's (transplanted) microbiota. Content is organized into:
1. Introduction
2. The recipient microbiome: a disrupted ecosystem (pre-transplant disruptions, immunoregulatory functions)
3. Loss of beneficial taxa and ecological fallout
4. The donor microbiome: a resilient influence (transplant dynamics, immune reconstitution)
5. The Microbiome-GVHD Axis (immune-microbiome interactions, SCFA signaling, Treg modulation)
6. Microbiota-based therapeutic interventions (probiotics, prebiotics, FMT, personalized strategies)
7. Future directions / conclusion
