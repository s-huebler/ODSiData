# Supplementary information

### Supplementary Table 1

Clinical studies of microbiota-targeted interventions in allogeneic HSCT summarised in Figure 3 (41 studies, 1983-2025). The main text gives no separate caption; the Figure 3 caption says 'Additional details about each study can be found in Supplemental Table 1.'

[Supplementary Table 1](../assets/supp_table/supplementary-table-1.csv)

Source: supplementary-table-1.csv.
Row 1 is the header (15 named columns, from First Author to Intervention Period); rows 2-42 are one study each; columns P onward and rows 43-88 are empty in the supplied file. Supplied as a Windows-1252 CSV (kgmi_a_2631224_sm2305.csv); re-encoded from Windows-1252 to UTF-8 before conversion, with no other change. Source typos are kept as supplied (e.g. 'Clincal Trial.gov ID', 'Lyama S', 'Waslh E', 'Gavrillaki E', 'TNF-?'). More typos are kept as supplied ('Reciepint microbial diveristy', 'rifaxmin', 'Escherrichia', 'aGHVD', 'steroid-fractory' and others). 'Adult' and 'Adults' are both used. The Storb 1983 row gives 'Matched (175 MRD)' for 130 patients and repeats it under Secondary Outcome(s). The 'Kaito S, 28154090, 2017' row (3 patients, FMT) corresponds to the study plotted in Figure 3 as Spindelboeck et al. 2017 (ref 95).
