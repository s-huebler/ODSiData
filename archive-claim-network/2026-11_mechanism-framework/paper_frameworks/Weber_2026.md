# Weber_2026 — paper framework

Full title: "Microbiome, GvHD, and immune reconstitution in allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation" (Weber, Tariq, Hazenberg, Poeck, Malard; Bone Marrow Transplantation, 2026, 61:385–394).

## Mechanism structure
Narrative review on how the intestinal microbiome shapes immune reconstitution and GvHD after alloHCT, organized around (1) risk factors driving dysbiosis and clinical impact, (2) interplay between microbiota and immune cell subsets (Tregs, Th17, ILC3, MDSCs via tryptophan–MDSC axis) plus gut-derived metabolites (SCFA, bile acids, tryptophan derivatives), (3) microbiome and immune reconstitution (specific commensals and metabolite producers linked to T/B cell recovery), (4) microbiome and GvHD (SCFA producers protective, Enterococcus / lactose-driven overgrowth pathogenic), and (5) microbiome-targeted strategies (antibiotic stewardship, prebiotics / RPS / GFO / resistant starch, probiotics / FMT including MaaT013, SER-155, postbiotics / sodium butyrate).

## Named models
- "Tryptophan–MDSC axis" — central pathway in which MDSC-expressed IDO degrades tryptophan into kynurenines, activating AhR to promote MDSC expansion, Treg induction, and GvHD attenuation while preserving GvL (p4).
