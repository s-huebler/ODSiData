# Moses_2026 — paper framework

Full title: "The gut microbiome in graft-versus-host disease: mechanisms of immune modulation and therapeutic approaches" (Moses & Yeh, Gut Microbes, 2026).

## Mechanism structure
Authors structure mechanisms around an augmented Ferrara three-phase model of GvHD pathogenesis, explicitly extending it with microbiota influence at each phase. Then they review therapeutic approaches.
