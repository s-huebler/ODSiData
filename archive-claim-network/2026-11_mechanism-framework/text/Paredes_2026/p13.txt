This table provides an overview of microbial-derived metabolites and their roles in the development and mitigation of graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) post allogeneic hematopoietic cell
transplantation (allo-HCT). It highlights key metabolites, the bacterial taxa that produce them and the substrates involved in their synthesis. In addition, the table lists the mechanisms of
action by which these metabolites regulate immune responses, maintain epithelial barrier integrity and suppress inflammation, as well as their therapeutic potential in alleviating GVHD. AhR,
aryl hydrocarbon receptor; BCAA, branched chain amino acid; CDCA, chenodeoxycholic acid; FDA, US Food and Drug Administration; FXR, farnesoid X receptor; GPCR, G-protein-coupled
receptor; HDAC, histone deacetylase; IL-22, interleukin-22; ILC3, group 3 innate lymphoid cell; NA, not applicable; PXR, pregnane X receptor; SCFA, short-chain fatty acid; TGR5, Takeda
G-protein-coupled receptor 5; TH1 cell, T helper 1 cell; Treg cell, regulatory T cell. aThese trials were selected and included to provide proof of concept for the potential use of these interventions
in the clinical setting of allo-HCT.
